# Balance Notes

## Priority Parameters

- Business cost vs expected profit
- Upgrade cost curve vs payback speed
- Debt pressure threshold vs risk growth
- Event cash swings vs starting cash
- Temporary modifier strength and duration
- Synergy bonuses vs standalone business returns

## First Things To Balance

- Early-game purchase access: turn 1-3 should not stall too often
- Debt usage: leverage should open options without becoming a default best play
- Upgrade timing: upgrades should compete with new purchases
- Real estate and energy unlock value vs knowledge cost

## Risky Values

- Any business with payback below 3 turns
- Debt cards that create positive cash loops with little downside
- Revenue buffs above +12% for 3 turns in the early game
- Expense reductions below -10% stacking too easily
- Synergies that outperform a whole extra business by themselves

## Metrics After 100 / 1000 Simulations

- Bankruptcy rate
- Completed 10-turn run rate
- Average and median final valuation
- Average turn reached
- Average ending cash
- Average ending debt
- Best and worst valuation

## MVP Targets

- Bankruptcy rate on normal difficulty: 15% to 35%
- Average completed turns: 7 to 9
- Full 10-turn completion should be achievable, not routine
- Debt should be useful but dangerous
- Buying a business should feel stronger than simply hoarding cash
- Synergies should be noticeable without breaking the economy
